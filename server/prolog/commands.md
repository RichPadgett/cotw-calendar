## Focus Item: Eating Unleavened Bread

**Requirement:** Eat unleavened bread for seven days during the Feast of Unleavened Bread. Remove leaven and do not eat leavened bread during the appointed feast.

**References:** Exodus 12:15, 18-20; Exodus 13:6-7; Leviticus 23:6; Numbers 28:17; Deuteronomy 16:3-4.
